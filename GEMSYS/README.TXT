GEMSYS - Speedo fonts for XGDOS
===============================

These eight Speedo outline fonts were donated by Bitstream Inc. to the
X Consortium and shipped with X11 (package font-bitstream-speedo 1.0.0).
They are not covered by XGDOS's MIT licence: they keep their own
Bitstream licence, in COPYING.TXT, which allows them to be used, copied,
modified, sublicensed, sold and redistributed as long as that notice
stays with them.

BITSTREAM CHARTER is a registered trademark of Bitstream Inc.

  FONT0648.SPD   Bitstream Charter
  FONT0649.SPD   Bitstream Charter Italic
  FONT0709.SPD   Bitstream Charter Black
  FONT0710.SPD   Bitstream Charter Black Italic
  FONT0419.SPD   Courier 10 Pitch
  FONT0582.SPD   Courier 10 Pitch Italic
  FONT0583.SPD   Courier 10 Pitch Bold
  FONT0611.SPD   Courier 10 Pitch Bold Italic

This is the Speedo font folder (FONTPATH in ASSIGN.SYS). Copy it to
C:\GEMSYS; the default ASSIGN.SYS enables all eight fonts. Put your own
.SPD fonts here too: XGDOSCFG lists new fonts it finds in the folder; tick
the ones you want and choose Save & Apply.
